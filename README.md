Golden Pancake Stack — a pure-Python-stdlib picture of a tall stack of golden pancakes on a white plate, melting butter and glossy maple syrup dripping down the sides, fresh blueberries and strawberries beside it, a dusting of powdered sugar, thin steam, and a small cup of black coffee blurred in the background. Warm morning light from the left over a dark wooden table, built to read delicious even at ~300 px.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output/entry.png`). No network, no third-party packages — the rasterizer (`draw.py`), the scene (`cakes.py`) and the PNG encoder (`png.py`) are all written from scratch on the Python standard library.
